# Photo credits

These files are stand-ins. They are not Bench & Trade students and not a Bench & Trade class. Set `business.photos.mode` to `"owned"` in `src/config/business.ts` and fill `ownedPhotos` in `src/config/photos.ts` when real class photos are ready.

The search was for five to eight photos of kids or teens doing woodworking or shop work, with safety glasses, ear protection, or an apron visible, and nothing unsafe in the frame. Free Pexels and Unsplash results did not yield that many. Other candidates were science-lab photos, kitchen or art-studio aprons, adult shop photos, Unsplash+ images that are not free, or scenes with saws, grinders, or bare hands at a blade. Those were not used. The apron in the first photo and the goggles in the second are the protection the publisher describes.
